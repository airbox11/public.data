Before amplifying: unlock the Outpost, upgrade both stations to IV, then upgrade the weapon to V with a module matching its base rarity: Common → Mk. I; Uncommon → Mk. II; Rare → Mk. III; Epic → Mk. IV; Legendary → Mk. V. Modules come from the Fallen Emperor (Emperor Gateway Conduit, Pendola Pass) or ARC Frigate cargo. Repairs require Amplified Fragments.
