OUTPOST FIELD GUIDE

More shelf space.
Know what fits.

Pick a shelf, cabinet, rack, or seat to see its attached slots and compatible items. Each group shares one compatible-item list. Recyclables show their material yields; trinkets show sell prices.

Attached storage slots are different from the room’s furniture placement slots. A large slot count does not mean a shelf can hold every item or crafting material.
