OUTPOST FIELD GUIDE

More shelf space.
Know what fits.

Pick a shelf, cabinet, rack, or seat to see its attached slots and compatible items. Or search an item to find every furniture piece that accepts it.

Attached storage slots are different from the room’s furniture placement slots. A large slot count does not mean a shelf can hold every item or crafting material.
